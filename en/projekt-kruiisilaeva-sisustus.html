What this work demands.

A ship fit-out comes from the client's drawings, as repeating modules, on someone else's schedule. Beside each demand is Halver's measured monthly production volume.

By drawing, not from a catalogue.

The client sends drawings and millimetre dimensions. Every part is sawn, edged and drilled to them.

approx. 45,000 m²

of panel material sawn per month

approx. 300,000 lin. m

edged per month

Repeating module, consistent batch.

Cabins and units repeat. The same element has to come out identical at the end of a batch as it was at the start.

Someone else's schedule.

The fit-out is installed to the client's schedule. Production has to fit inside a time window that someone else sets.

6,000,000+

holes drilled per month

What Halver makes.

Two companies, one production in Kose and Haljala.

Halver Mööbel makes made-to-order furniture to the client's drawings and requirements: fit-out for offices, shops, homes, ships and hotels.

Halver Arendus produces upholstery frames, kitchen furniture and furniture components. Because both companies share the same production, the visible furniture and the frame behind it come out of one and the same production.
