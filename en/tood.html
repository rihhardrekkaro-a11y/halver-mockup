The sectors Halver works in.

Each one states what that kind of order demands from a furniture manufacturer and what Halver makes for it. All five come out of the same Kose and Haljala production, off the same machines.

01

Cruise ship fit-out

A ship fit-out comes from the client's drawings and as repeating modules: cabins and units repeat, and the same element has to come out identical at the end of a batch as it was at the start. Installation runs to the client's schedule, so production has to fit inside a time window that someone else sets.

What Halver makes
Made-to-measure fit-out elements to the client's drawings and dimensions
Delivered by
Halver Mööbel OÜ
See what this order demands from production
02

Hotel fit-out

A hotel room is a module that repeats floor after floor: the same furniture in quantity, at a consistent standard. The public spaces are the opposite: one-off pieces, different on every site. Both come off the same drawings and both have to be ready on the site's schedule.

What Halver makes
Made-to-order fit-out to the client's drawings, both the repeating room elements and the one-off pieces
Delivered by
Halver Mööbel OÜ
03

Kitchens

A kitchen is the densest piece of furniture in the building: cabinets, appliances, sink and worktop all have to meet a room that is already built. The photographs above are from Halver's own projects.

What Halver makes
Kitchen furniture, tall cabinets, worktops and wall panels; as a made-to-order project and as components for kitchen manufacturers
Delivered by
Halver Mööbel OÜ · Halver Arendus OÜ
04

Offices

Office fit-out is a repeating element in quantity: workstations, storage, meeting rooms, a reception desk. Installation runs to the building's handover schedule. Volume and deadline matter here as much as the detail does.

What Halver makes
Made-to-measure office fit-out to the client's drawings
Delivered by
Halver Mööbel OÜ
05

Shops

Retail fit-out has to take wear and repeat the same solution from one location to the next. Counters, shelving and back-of-house furniture come from the client's drawings and go in inside a short window.

What Halver makes
Made-to-measure retail and counter furniture to the client's drawings
Delivered by
Halver Mööbel OÜ
