Machines, by name.

The main machines in production, from panel cutting to the finished part.

  1. 01 · Panel saw

    Holzma HPL 430

    The panel is cut to size against a cutting plan. This is where every part's path through production begins.

  2. 02 · Edgebanding line

    Homag EDGETEQ S-500

    The edges of cut parts are covered with edge banding. Both straight and specially shaped parts run through the same line.

  3. 03 · CNC machining centre

    Weeke BHX 500

    Drilling and milling in one machine, one part at a time, to programme.

  4. 04 · Drilling centre

    Skipper 130

    Repeat parts are drilled in batches. An operator loads panels onto the line and finished parts are stacked.

  5. 05 · Conveyor line

    Omal

    A conveyor carries parts under two upright processing units.
